# Neon Dominos

Included solo game: a three-round Neon Draw match against your rival. Uses the existing shared Godot4.7.2 engine, canonical toolbar/dialogs, purple loader, audio buses/preferences and owner-scoped save/return flow. It is preinstalled in the included catalog, with original neon domino artwork, preserving existing owner ordering and Panda's premiere behavior. The native start menu offers the solo route. A short objective dialog with a Play button opens each new match. It is excluded from contact/friend shortcuts; the broker explicitly refuses a friend token. No SMS protocol or new permission is added.

## Complete rules

A double-six set has28 unique tiles. Each player receives seven;14 remain in the draw pile. Each round opens with the highest double in either hand, or the highest pip sum when no double is dealt. The selected opening tile is highlighted. The wrapping rack keeps every tile visible without scrolling, including the maximum21-tile hand. Tap a playable tile to place it immediately when the end is unambiguous. If both different ends match, select the cyan or pink end on the actual table. Alternatively drag a tile onto a matching end; release outside the table to cancel. Orientation is automatic. The chain has two ends and no spinners. Unplayable tiles are dimmed; legal choices glow cyan. Newly drawn tiles glow gold and feedback identifies both faces.

Draw one tile at a time only when no legal play exists. Pass only with an empty draw pile and no legal play. Each pip played earns one point. A round ends when either hand is empty or both players pass. The pip value of each player's remaining tiles is deducted from their own score. The higher net score wins the round. After three rounds, the higher combined net score wins the match. Equal scores draw. Play again starts a new match. No wagers, purchases, advertising, fake rewards or network service.

Your rival's bounded heuristic receives only its own hand, the public chain and required opener. It balances pip disposal, doubles and future connections. It does not inspect the player's hidden rack. Model actions commit and checkpoint before presentation. A restored pending animation is adopted as an already committed turn; no scoring or audio replays. Saved state validates numeric JSON fields, all28 unique tile identities, pip faces/orientation/joins, hand/draw bounds, turn and round state, terminal awards and previous round totals. Old saves keep the current deal as round one because earlier net scores cannot be recovered from round wins. State and particles are bounded.
