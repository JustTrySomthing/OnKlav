# Clock, Flashlight and Camera —0.99.0 / code107

## Implementation

All three are included signed SDK packages, not separate Android APKs. AppSdk registers them; Catalog and SdkDesktopIcons supply theme-tinted artwork; SdkAppScreen dispatches their native Compose workspaces. Shared OnKlav navigation remains the operating-system back action. Original publisher and Android identities are retained.

Clock: Alarms / Timer / Stopwatch. Up to32 named alarms, weekly days, one-shot times, snooze, system sound selection and vibration; local-time/DST scheduling with exact AlarmManager alarm-clock events. Timers use monotonic deadlines and saved wall deadlines across boots, with pause/resume/reset and quick presets. Stopwatch state and lap splits persist; lap export uses explicit sharing access. ClockReceiver rejects stale events and restores future schedules after reboot/update/time changes. ClockRingService supplies alarm audio focus, looping sound, vibration, wake lock and a notification with Snooze/Dismiss; ClockAlarmActivity supplies themed ringing controls. Full-screen and precise alarm access remain Android-controlled. Recovery does not start playback from boot.

Flashlight: a large LED power control, steady/SOS, auto-off and hardware strength where Android/API/device capabilities support it. The camera-type foreground service has an Off notification and checks live grants. Torch callbacks report unavailable hardware or competing camera use. Screen-light mode offers white/warm/red and screen brightness, restoring the window state on exit. No rapid strobe or pretend hardware brightness is presented. Camera and SDK torch leases are released before acquiring the camera.

Camera: lifecycle-bound CameraX1.5.3 preview, photos and video, front/rear, pinch/slider zoom, tap focus, supported exposure compensation, photo flash/video light, countdown, grid, selfie mirroring, photo ratios and quality. Video qualities are restricted to reported hardware support, with microphone permission/control, pause/resume/stop and measured duration/size. Leaving the app finalizes recording and releases the camera. Orientation updates capture targets. Native-camera session operations require the Camera package and its live grants, and scope review/trash to its own active captures. Photos save to Images/Camera and video to Video/Camera; files are streamed rather than held in memory. Media imports and exports allow2GiB; failed saves remain private pending captures with Retry/Discard controls. Shared captures survive app removal.
