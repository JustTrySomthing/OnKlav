# SHROOM 0.2.5 combat balance

Enemies acquire players within a local sight radius, lose pursuit after a short sight break, and stop following the player into the entry airlock. Their speed stays below ordinary player movement, and close enemies back off instead of continuing to steer into the player. Close-range shots use a forward-only check that respects cover, including when the camera sits above the target's capsule.

The spitter slot is now a low gelatinous spore blob with a wide body, squash animation, a glowing charge tell and a ranged spacing pattern. It appears in the first sector's hub. Existing spitter save records map to the blob. It retreats when approached and uses a slower, telegraphed spit attack.

OnKlav 0.100.75 includes the same soundtrack selection and preserves the installed app identity.
