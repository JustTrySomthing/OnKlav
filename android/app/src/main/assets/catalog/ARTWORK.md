# Game catalog artwork

The seven built-in game icons use transparent WebP artwork, fitted inside their catalog slots. The six refreshed cutouts depict the Crash Test Panda cannon, Air Hockey strikers and puck, Cats vs Dogs portraits, Naval Battle ship, Blackjack cards and chips, and Neon Dominos tiles. Shroom uses its separate glossy red mushroom cutout. The cutouts were made with OpenAI image generation.

The Cats vs Dogs in-game tabby token uses a burgundy inset and a rose-red rim to separate its silhouette from the coin. It is distinct from the catalog icon.

Last Light Express uses a transparent 344 × 344 WebP. The teal railcar and lantern courier are rendered at a three-quarter angle to match the depth and framing of the other Games catalog cutouts.
